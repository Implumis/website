## To a New Beginning

### What is Implumis?

Hey there, and welcome to our website! I believe it would be ideal to start by explaining what Implumis is.

Me (Charles) and my friend Eliott enrolled into mechanical engineering, and we wanted to have something to work on throughout our studies. Of course, we can get ourselves involved into the already existing clubs at our University. Although, that is a whole lot different than Implumis.

By going our own way we have the advantage of owning everything we make. This is great because it means self-custody. This is also bad because it means funding from our own pockets, a smaller team and overall just a more difficult time scaling up. We know and this is partly why we want to do this. We love the challenge of the entrepreneurial aspect that comes with it.

The next step was to choose what that project would be. We had already thought of making a drone launcher for fun in the past and so making a UAV project seemed like the right direction. And that is just what Implumis is all about. Making UAVs.

Of course we have brainstormed exactly what we would like the product to be down the line, and so far, we figured it would be a long-range data collection drone. Our ultimate goal isn't to just make a drone. We aim to innovate through at least one area of the product.

## Branding

Before we even picked a name we had figured out a logo. Usually it is the other way around, but we were already inspired. Initially I made an eagle's leg clawing at a gear, but Eliott disliked the idea:

![First logo attempt|left|400px|First logo proposal](/images/documents/baby_steps/first_logo_attempt.avif)

I then suggested we use the [snowy owl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowy_owl) since it is the national bird of Quebec (our province). We then both worked on multiple iterations of a logo in that spirit until we landed on the final one we liked:

![Logo iterations|left|100%|Logo iterations from left to right](/images/documents/baby_steps/logo_iterations.avif)

Now we were just missing a name. While waiting for my next class at school I was trying to find something related to the logo, and so I looked up the scientific name of the snowy owl. While reading it, I realized I could try to translate some words into latin and that's when I thought of "featherless". I looked up the translation and found that it was implūmis:

> Wiktionary.org → [implūmis](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/implumis): featherless, without feathers.

## What is Next?

We obviously won't begin by making exact drone we want from the get go. Neither me nor Eliott have comprehensive experience with aeronotics. We plan on beginning with basic FPV prototypes until we feel confident enough to start working on the actual implementation.

As we make progress we'll publish further updates here so stay tuned!
